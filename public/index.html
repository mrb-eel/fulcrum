Evidence register

Supported analysis and limits

Linear holdings, lending supply marks and collateralized loans can be evaluated from the common import format. Automatic wallet discovery currently covers Morpho Blue only. LPs, options, perps, bridges and recursive vault shares require specific adapters; unsupported records remain listed and are excluded from totals.

Quotes, control maps and oracle traces are never copied from one market to another. The original public case has deeper evidence than a newly discovered market. Imports are treated as supplied claims, not independent verification. No scenario is a forecast, VaR estimate or transaction recommendation.
