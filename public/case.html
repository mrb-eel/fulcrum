What this study covers—and what still needs work

One public Ethereum position. It is not identified as Belem Capital’s portfolio. The contract trace covers the selected oracle configuration. It does not audit every Ethena, custody, exchange or governance dependency.

The shocks are assumptions. No event probability is estimated. The model does not simulate interest accrual, liquidation auctions, an oracle switch over time, or an executable loan unwind.
